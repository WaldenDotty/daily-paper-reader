## 摘要
当一个动力系统的演化方程显式依赖于时间时，该系统被称为非自治系统。在探索此类系统的各种几何方法中，余辛表述为时间相关哈密顿系统提供了扩展辛几何的自然框架。然而，在数值离散化过程中保持相关的几何结构仍然是一个具有挑战性的问题：标准积分器（如显式欧拉格式）通常无法保持余辛体积或底层泊松结构。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种基于广义正则变换的非自治哈密顿系统离散化的几何方法。该方法在扩展相空间T^*(Q × R)上构建了一个辛同胚，其投影到T^*Q × R定义了一个保持结构的离散流。我们表明该公式保证了关键不变量的保持，包括体积形式、泊松括号以及每个时间纤维上的辛结构。

## Abstract
A dynamical system is said to be \emph{non-autonomous} when the differential equations describing its evolution depends explicitly on time. Among the various geometric approaches to investigate such systems, the cosymplectic formulation provides a natural framework that extends symplectic geometry to time-dependent Hamiltonians systems. However, preserving the associated geometric structures under numerical discretization remains a challenging problem: standard integrators, such as explicit Euler schemes, generally fail to conserve the cosymplectic volume or the underlying Poisson structure.   In this work we propose a geometric method for the discretization of non-autonomous Hamiltonian systems based on \emph{generalized canonical transformations}. The approach constructs a symplectomorphism on the extended phase space $T^*(Q \times \mathbb{R})$ whose projection onto $T^*Q \times \mathbb{R}$ defines a structure-preserving discrete flow. We show that this formulation guarantees the preservation of key invariants, including the volume form, the Poisson bracket, and the symplectic structure on each time fiber.
