## 摘要
动态最优输运将最优输运、流体力学和梯度流理论统一在一个连续动力学框架内，为物理学、生物学和机器学习中的应用提供了具有几何感知的语言。然而，传统形式化方法将其描述为必须显式满足连续性方程的约束优化问题，阻碍了直接从数据中重构底层动力学。我们提出了动态最优输运的能量变分方法（EVMDOT），通过结合流映射、最小作用量原理和最大耗散原理，在能量变分框架内重新表述该问题。流映射通过自动满足连续性方程将约束问题转化为无约束问题，而保守力与耗散力之间的平衡决定了速度场。应用于Fokker-Planck方程时，EVMDOT直接从时间序列密度数据重构能量景观和Waddington景观。通过数值实验，我们揭示EVMDOT在数据量与数据质量之间实现了内在平衡：充足的数据量可以补偿有限的数据质量，使得重构对观测窗口的选择具有鲁棒性。我们进一步将EVMDOT应用于阿尔茨海默病神经影像学倡议（ADNI）数据集，推断淀粉样蛋白-β和tau蛋白的势能景观，揭示对应认知正常和阿尔茨海默病阶段的两个势阱以及它们之间的转变路径。

## Abstract
Dynamic optimal transport unifies optimal transport, fluid mechanics, and gradient-flow theory within a continuous dynamical framework, offering a geometry-aware language for applications across physics, biology, and machine learning. However, conventional formulations cast it as a constrained optimization problem that must explicitly satisfy the continuity equation, hindering the reconstruction of the underlying dynamics directly from data. We propose the energetic variational method for dynamic optimal transport (EVMDOT), which reformulates it within an energetic variational framework by combining the flow map, the least action principle, and the maximum dissipation principle. The flow map recasts the constrained problem as an unconstrained one by automatically enforcing the continuity equation, while the balance between the conservative and dissipative forces determines the velocity field. Applied to the Fokker--Planck equation, the EVMDOT reconstructs both the energy landscape and the Waddington landscape directly from time-series density data. Through numerical experiments, we reveal that the EVMDOT achieves an intrinsic balance between data quantity and data quality: a sufficient data quantity compensates for limited data quality, making the reconstruction robust to the choice of the observation window. We further apply the EVMDOT to the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) dataset to infer the potential landscape of amyloid-$β$ and tau, revealing two wells corresponding to the cognitively normal and Alzheimer's disease stages and the transition pathway between them.
