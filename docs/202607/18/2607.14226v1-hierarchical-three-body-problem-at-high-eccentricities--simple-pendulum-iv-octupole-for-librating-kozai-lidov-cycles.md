## 摘要
我们解析求解了双平均受限层级三体问题中平动Kozai-Lidov循环（即Kozai常数为负，近心点幅角平动的循环）的长期八极矩演化。与旋转循环一样，当轨道角动量的法向分量消失时，平动循环达到极端偏心率，但它们的慢速动力学此前尚未解决：正如Katz、Dong和Malhotra（2011）所指出的，偏心率矢量的方位角每周期跳变半圈，因此主导阶八极矩贡献交替变号并成对抵消。我们证明，在八极矩强度的二阶上，留存动力学是一个具有显式系数的简单摆。该摆预测法向角动量的慢振荡，其振幅与八极矩强度成线性关系，演化时间尺度约为长期时间尺度除以八极矩强度，同时还给出了轨道翻转的显式判据。对于层级三体系统中典型的八极矩强度，只要等待相应更长时间尺度，这些振幅可与旋转循环的振幅相当，并且在平动窗口的部分区域可超过后者。该解析模型在平动窗口内与双平均方程的数值积分一致，针对研究偏心Kozai-Lidov效应（从热木星形成到引力波源）的层级三体系统中典型的八极矩强度。

## Abstract
We solve analytically the long-term octupole evolution of \textit{librating} Kozai-Lidov cycles - those with a negative Kozai constant, in which the argument of pericenter librates - in the double-averaged restricted hierarchical three-body problem. Librating cycles reach extreme eccentricities when the normal component of the orbital angular momentum vanishes, just as rotating cycles do, but their slow dynamics was left unsolved: as noted by Katz, Dong \& Malhotra (2011), the azimuth of the eccentricity vector jumps by half a turn every cycle, so the leading-order octupole kick alternates in sign and cancels pairwise. We show that the surviving dynamics, at second order in the octupole strength, is a simple pendulum with explicit coefficients. The pendulum predicts slow oscillations of the normal angular momentum with amplitude linear in the octupole strength, evolving on a timescale of order the secular timescale divided by the octupole strength, together with an explicit criterion for orbital flips. At octupole strengths typical of hierarchical triples, these amplitudes are comparable to - and in part of the librating window can exceed - those of rotating cycles, provided one waits the correspondingly longer timescale. The analytic model agrees with numerical integrations of the double-averaged equations across the librating window, for octupole strengths typical of the hierarchical triples in which the eccentric Kozai-Lidov effect is studied - from hot-Jupiter formation to gravitational-wave sources.
