## 摘要
我们解决了在观测窗口内利用低地球轨道卫星收集的多普勒频率测量数据对地面静止物联网无线电发射器进行被动定位的问题。该问题具有挑战性，因为低成本物联网设备的无线电发射受到各种复合测量误差源的影响，这些误差源共同使得似然函数难以以闭合形式处理。因此，我们应用并研究了近似贝叶斯计算（ABC）方法在此任务中的性能。数值结果展示了两种ABC方法（拒绝采样ABC和序贯蒙特卡洛ABC）的统计和计算性能。

## Abstract
We address the problem of passive localisation of a stationary, ground-level IoT radio emitter using Doppler frequency measurements collected by low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellites during an observation window. The problem is challenging because radio emission from low-cost IoT devices is affected by various compounding sources of measurement error, that collectively render the likelihood function intractable in a closed form. Hence, we apply and investigate the performance of Approximate Bayesian Computation (ABC) methods for this task. Numerical results demonstrate the statistical and computational performance of two ABC methods, rejection sampling ABC and sequential Monte Carlo ABC.
