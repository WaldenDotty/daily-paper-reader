## 摘要
给定一个具有低维吸引子的遍历动力系统的标量可观测值，两类方法重构并预测潜在状态：基于递归的方法（类比法及其衍生方法），等待轨迹返回先前观测状态的$\varepsilon$邻域；以及基于观测器的方法，在延迟重构上拟合收敛的状态估计器。我们形式化并经验验证了二者之间的指数级分离：递归的期望代价按$\varepsilon^{-d}$缩放，其中$d$是不变测度的点态维数（Kac引理和定量庞加莱递归的结果），而可检测的线性观测器在$Θ(\log(1/\varepsilon)/(1-ρ(A_{cl})^2))$步内收敛，其中$ρ(A_{cl})$是Riccati不动点的闭环谱半径。两个定律均通过数值验证（洛伦兹吸引子上返回时间指数$-1.8$，理论为$-2.05$；观测器代价在$\log(1/\varepsilon)$上呈线性，$R^2=1.000$，在$(1-ρ^2)^{-1}$上呈线性，$R^2=0.985$），在$\varepsilon=10^{-6}$、$d\approx 2$时测量代价差距约为$10^{9}$。我们用一个接纳协议（Kac-Riccati门）补充该定理，通过替代数据预测门控判断信号是否属于定理类别；它还解释了“通用”分形维数的民间传说作为数据集大小伪影，受限于$2\log_{10}N$。在真实数据上，该门接纳圣塔菲激光基准（$\hat D_2=2.0$）并拒绝月度太阳黑子序列，再现了历史低维数主张的既定解决方案。所有结果通过单个验证脚本可复现（17/17项检查）。

## Abstract
Given a scalar observable of an ergodic dynamical system with a low-dimensional attractor, two families of methods reconstruct and predict the underlying state: recurrence-based methods (the method of analogues and its descendants), which wait for the trajectory to return to an $\varepsilon$-neighborhood of a previously observed state, and observer-based methods, which fit a converging state estimator on the delay reconstruction. We formalize and empirically verify an exponential separation between the two: the expected cost of recurrence scales as $\varepsilon^{-d}$, where $d$ is the pointwise dimension of the invariant measure (a consequence of the Kac lemma and quantitative Poincare recurrence), whereas a detectable linear observer converges in $Θ(\log(1/\varepsilon)/(1-ρ(A_{cl})^2))$ steps, where $ρ(A_{cl})$ is the closed-loop spectral radius of the Riccati fixed point. Both laws are verified numerically (return-time exponent $-1.8$ on the Lorenz attractor against the theoretical $-2.05$; observer cost linear in $\log(1/\varepsilon)$ with $R^2=1.000$ and in $(1-ρ^2)^{-1}$ with $R^2=0.985$), yielding a measured cost gap of $\sim 10^{9}$ at $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ for $d\approx 2$. We complement the theorem with an admission protocol (the Kac-Riccati gate) deciding whether a signal lies inside the theorem's class, via surrogate-data prediction gating; it also explains the folklore of "universal" fractal dimensions as a dataset-size artifact bounded by $2\log_{10}N$. On real data the gate admits the Santa Fe laser benchmark ($\hat D_2=2.0$) and refuses the monthly sunspot series, reproducing the settled resolution of historical low-dimensionality claims. All results reproduce from a single verification script (17/17 checks).
