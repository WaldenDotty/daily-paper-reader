## 摘要
在结构模型不确定性下的非线性状态估计仍然是自主制导、导航与控制（GNC）系统的核心挑战。经典估计器使用假设动力学传播状态，并通过后验校正融入测量，在模型失配时会导致新息有偏、估计器不一致，以及由于提议-似然失配导致的粒子退化。本文提出了几何投影粒子滤波器（GPF），这是一种测量信息引导的框架，其中观测几何直接影响状态传播。该方法将名义漂移动力学投影到测量一致子空间，强制动力学与观测之间的局部兼容性。这产生了几何一致的提议过程，在加权之前减少了有效失配，同时通过测度变换保留了贝叶斯后验。发展了连续时间公式，引入了一个几何协态变量，该变量表征了失配引起的不一致性，并在正确建模下具有零条件均值。证明滤波误差由正交于测量一致子空间的失配分量控制，并建立了粒子一致性，收敛速度为标准蒙特卡洛速率。该方法在具有部分可观测性和持续失配的月球下降导航场景中进行了评估。结果表明，在标准粒子滤波器发散的情况下，该方法显著减少了粒子退化，保持了有效样本量，并限制了估计误差，实现了高达一个数量级的精度提升。这些发现表明，在传播过程中强制几何兼容性为改善模型不确定性下非线性粒子滤波的鲁棒性和一致性提供了一种原则性机制。

## Abstract
Nonlinear state estimation under structural model uncertainty remains a central challenge in autonomous Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC) systems. Classical estimators propagate states using assumed dynamics and incorporate measurements through posterior correction, which under mismatch leads to biased innovations, estimator inconsistency, and particle degeneracy due to proposal--likelihood mismatch.   This paper presents the \emph{Geometric Projection Particle Filter (GPF)}, a measurement-informed framework in which observation geometry directly influences state propagation. The method projects nominal drift dynamics onto the measurement-consistent subspace, enforcing local compatibility between dynamics and observations. This yields a geometry-consistent proposal process that reduces effective mismatch prior to weighting while preserving the Bayesian posterior via a change-of-measure formulation.   A continuous-time formulation is developed, introducing a geometric co-state variable that characterizes mismatch-induced inconsistency and has zero conditional mean under correct modeling. It is shown that filtering error is governed by the component of mismatch orthogonal to the measurement-consistent subspace, and particle consistency is established with convergence at the standard Monte Carlo rate.   The approach is evaluated on lunar descent navigation scenarios with partial observability and persistent mismatch. Results demonstrate significant reduction in particle degeneracy, sustained effective sample size, and bounded estimation error in regimes where standard particle filters diverge, achieving up to an order-of-magnitude improvement in accuracy.   These findings show that enforcing geometric compatibility during propagation provides a principled mechanism for improving robustness and consistency in nonlinear particle filtering under model uncertainty.
