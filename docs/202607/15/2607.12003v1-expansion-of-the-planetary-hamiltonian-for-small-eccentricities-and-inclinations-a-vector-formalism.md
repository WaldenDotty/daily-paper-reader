## 摘要
我们重新审视了经典的行星哈密顿量在小偏心率和小轨道相互倾角下的展开，提出了一种完全基于矢量形式的推导。我们证明了扰动函数的长期部分以及任何其他傅里叶谐波都可以用位于系统不变平面内的矢量的标量积来表示。此外，我们还展示了如何利用轨道的角动量和偏心率矢量来表达任何此类项。

## Abstract
We revisit the classical expansion of the planetary Hamiltonian for small eccentricities and mutual inclinations of the orbits, presenting a derivation based entirely on vector formalism. We demonstrate that the secular part of the disturbing function, as well as any other Fourier harmonic, can be expressed in terms of scalar products of vectors lying in the system's invariant plane. Furthermore, we show how to express any such term using the angular momentum and eccentricity vectors of the orbits.
