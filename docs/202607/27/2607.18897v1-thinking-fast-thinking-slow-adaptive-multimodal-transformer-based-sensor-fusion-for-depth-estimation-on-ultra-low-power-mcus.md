## 摘要
基于人工智能（AI）的多模态传感器融合是超低功耗（ULP）嵌入式和网络物理系统中日益受到关注的相关话题，因为它能在实际约束下提高可靠性、准确性和鲁棒性。然而，在超受限的低于100毫瓦平台上增加更多传感器需要平衡能耗与预测精度。为实现这一宏伟目标，我们提出了一种新颖的自适应AI方法，该方法将多模态传感器融合（摄像头、超声波和飞行时间传感器）与轻量级递归Transformer架构（688k参数）相结合。我们通过一种机制处理深度图估计任务，该机制结合了跨迭代的令牌传播和增量传感器利用。在每次迭代中，基于置信度的门控机制动态决定是否通过逐步添加更丰富但功耗更高的传感器作为输入来继续计算。令牌传播通过跨时间转发上下文特征确保时间一致性。为部署我们的算法并测试首个实际原型，我们设计了一款新型印刷电路板，集成了所有三种传感器，并搭配超低功耗GWT GAP9多核片上系统。当将我们的自适应系统与使用所有传感器和迭代的相同流水线在NYUv2数据集上进行比较时，我们仅损失了4.8%的δ1精度，却实现了90%的能效节省（每帧2.44毫焦耳）。最后，我们的自适应方法尽管使用的参数减少了9倍，但与MobileDepth相比，δ1精度仅低5.6%。与同样在GAP9上运行的最新模型相比，我们的方法通过自适应传感器融合在相同平均功耗预算（约400毫瓦）内将δ1精度提高了31.8%。

## Abstract
Artificial intelligence (AI)-based multimodal sensor fusion is a relevant topic gaining ever more traction across ultra-low-power (ULP) embedded and cyber-physical systems, as it improves reliability, accuracy, and robustness under real-world constraints. However, adding more and more sensors to ultra-constrained sub-100 mW platforms requires balancing energy consumption against prediction accuracy. To achieve this ambitious goal, we present a novel adaptive AI methodology that combines multimodal sensor fusion (camera, ultrasound, and Time-of-Flight sensors) with a lightweight recurrent Transformer-based architecture (688 k parameters). We address the depth map estimation task with a mechanism that combines token propagation across iterations with incremental sensor utilization. At each iteration, a confidence-based gating mechanism dynamically decides whether to continue the computation by adding progressively richer but more power-demanding sensors as input. Token propagation ensures temporal consistency by forwarding context features across time. To deploy our algorithm and test a first real-world prototype, we design a novel printed circuit board featuring all three sensors, coupled with an ULP GWT GAP9 multicore System-on-Chip. When comparing our adaptive system against the same pipeline using all sensors and iterations on the NYUv2 dataset, we lose only 4.8% of the δ1 accuracy in exchange for 90% energy saving (2.44 mJ/frame). Finally, our adaptive method marks only 5.6% lower δ1 accuracy than MobileDepth despite using 9x fewer parameters. Compared with a state-of-the-art model also running on GAP9, our method improves δ1 accuracy by 31.8% thanks to our adaptive sensor fusion while operating within the same average power budget (~400 mW).
