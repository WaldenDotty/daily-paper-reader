## 摘要
我们继续由Kilin ({\em Reg. Chaot. Dyn.} 4, (1999)) 以及Martínez和Simó ({\em Celest. Mech. Dynam. Astronom.} 128, (2017)) 发起的关于常曲率二维空间中限制性三体问题相对平衡的分类与稳定性的研究，该研究推广了平面问题中的经典拉格朗日点。在将问题表述为具有两个自由度的自治拉格朗日系统后（其唯一参数为主星的曲率κ和质量比μ），我们通过将分析方法与计算机辅助证明相结合，为κ>0的情况建立了若干分类结果。这些结果为先前仅具有数值证据的现象提供了严谨确认。我们还为正曲率中相对平衡行为与平面及负曲率情形中观察到行为之间的定性差异提供了拓扑解释。我们的分析聚焦于小μ的区间，表明正曲率对三角平衡点L4和L5具有稳定作用，而负曲率似乎具有相反效果。

## Abstract
We continue the study initiated by Kilin ({\em Reg. Chaot. Dyn.} 4, (1999)) and by Martínez and Simó ({\em Celest. Mech. Dynam. Astronom.} 128, (2017)) on the classification and stability of the relative equilibria of the restricted three-body problem in two-dimensional spaces of constant curvature, which generalize the classical Lagrange points of the planar problem. After formulating the problem as an autonomous Lagrangian system with two degrees of freedom, whose only parameters are the curvature $κ$ and the mass ratio $μ$ of the primaries, we establish several classification results for the case $κ>0$ by combining analytical methods with computer-assisted proofs. These results provide rigorous confirmation of phenomena for which previously only numerical evidence was available. We also provide topological explanations for the qualitative differences between the behavior of relative equilibria in positive curvature and that observed in the planar and negative-curvature cases. Our analysis focuses on the regime of small $μ$ and indicates that positive curvature has a stabilizing effect on the triangular equilibria $\Ll_4$ and $\Ll_5$, whereas negative curvature appears to have the opposite effect.
