## 摘要
太阳系天体之间的精确时间尺度变换是深空科学和太阳系导航的一项基本需求。将天体中心坐标时 TCX 与太阳系质心坐标时 TCB 联系起来的后牛顿相对论表达式依赖于目标天体的引力环境和速度，并且必须与所选行星历表一致地进行计算。迄今为止，只有 INPOP 在其历表发布中提供了这样的解，但也仅针对地球给出了 TCG-TCB。我们提出 TEMPUS，这是一种利用任意历表给出的位置和速度，对太阳系任意天体的 IAU 2000 时间变换进行数值积分的工具。我们提供了经过验证且不依赖于特定历表的 TCX-TCB 实现，并对照已发表的独立解评估其精度。速率 $d\,\mathrm{TCX}/d\,\mathrm{TCB}$ 采用 12 阶 Adams-Moulton 预测-校正法进行积分。这里测试的引力势求和包括太阳、八大行星、冥王星、最多 343 颗主带小行星以及 30 个海王星外天体。输出可以存储为可读格式的采样值表格，或存储为切比雪夫系数文件。我们与 TU Dresden 的 Klioner 时间解、INPOP19a 和 INPOP21a 提供的 TCG-TCB 解，以及 LTE440 月球解进行了比较。对于行星天体，TEMPUS 在 200 年时间内与 Klioner 在 $10^{-21} s\,s^{-1}$ 量级上一致。与 INPOP19a 发布的 TCG-TCB 相比，我们也达到相同量级的一致性。主带小行星以及海王星外天体会在 $10^{-18} s\,s^{-1}$ 量级上改变行星时间尺度，而行星历表和月球历表的选择也会带来 $10^{-18} s\,s^{-1}$ 量级的差异。TEMPUS 为太阳系任意天体提供了一种经过验证、不依赖特定历表的后牛顿时间变换。

## Abstract
Precise time-scale transformations between solar-system bodies are an essential requirement for deep-space science and solar system navigation. The post-Newtonian relativistic expression relating a body-centered coordinate time TCX to the Barycentric Coordinate Time TCB depends on the gravitational environment and velocity of the target body, and must be evaluated consistently with the chosen planetary ephemerides. So far only INPOP has provided such a solution as part of its ephemerides releases, albeit only for Earth with TCG-TCB. We present TEMPUS, a tool that numerically integrates the IAU 2000 time-transformation for any solar-system body using positions and velocities from an arbitrary ephemerides. We provide a validated, ephemerides-agnostic implementation of TCX-TCB and assess its accuracy against published independent solutions. The rate $d\,\mathrm{TCX}/d\,\mathrm{TCB}$ is integrated with an Adams-Moulton order-12 predictor-corrector. The gravitational potential sum tested here includes the Sun, eight planets, Pluto, up to 343 main-belt asteroids, and 30 trans-Neptunian objects. Outputs can be stored in readable format tables of sampled values or as Chebyshev coefficient files. Comparisons are performed against the TU Dresden Klioner time solutions, the TCG-TCB solution provided with INPOP19a and INPOP21a, and the LTE440 lunar solution. For the planetary bodies TEMPUS agrees with Klioner at the $10^{-21} s\,s^{-1}$ level over 200 yr. Against the INPOP19a released TCG-TCB we also agree to the same level. The main-belt asteroids as well as the trans-Neptunian objects shift the planetary time scales at the $10^{-18} s\,s^{-1}$ level, and the choice of planetary and lunar ephemerides contributes differences on the $10^{-18} s\,s^{-1}$ level as well. TEMPUS provides a validated, ephemeris-agnostic post-Newtonian time-transformation for any solar-system body.
