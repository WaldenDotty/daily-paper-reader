## 摘要
三维地月转移通常比平面转移消耗更多燃料，但根据发射场纬度和最终月球轨道的期望倾角，它们是必要的。对最优三维转移进行系统搜索是一项真正的挑战，因为需要跨越的参数集维度很大。在本文中，我们提出了一种新的三维转移设计方法，对点火采用切向速度约束，并基于函数连接理论（Theory of Functional Connections）使用超定约束技术进行建模，从而以数值高效的方式对解进行最优搜索。所开发的方法在此应用于最优三维地月转移的系统研究，但也可用于设计任何类型的三维机动。

## Abstract
Three dimensional Earth-Moon transfers usually consume more fuel than planar ones, but are needed depending on the latitude of the launch site and the desired inclination of the final lunar orbit. A systematic search of the optimal 3D transfers represents a true challenge due the dimension of the parameter set to span. In this paper, we propose a new method to design 3D transfers using a tangential velocity constraint for the burns, modeled using the overdetermined constraints technique based on the Theory of Functional Connections for an optimal search of solutions in a numerically efficient way. The developed method is applied here for a systematic study of optimal 3D Earth-Moon transfers, but could be used to design any type of 3D maneuvers.
