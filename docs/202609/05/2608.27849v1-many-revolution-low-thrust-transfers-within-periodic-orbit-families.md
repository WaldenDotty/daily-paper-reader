## 摘要
本文探讨了利用简化流形空间初始化方法，生成一族三体周期轨道之间的时间和燃料最优的多次旋转低推力转移。平动点轨道族通过多段切比雪夫多项式和一维傅里叶级数近似。控制被映射到族空间中，并在族空间动力学中使用一维目标方案生成相空间的初始猜测，直接考虑缠绕数。然后使用基于积分配点的直接方法，首先将可行轨迹延续到相空间中，然后针对特定代价函数进行优化。该方法首先应用于远距离逆行轨道族。随后讨论了时间正则化动力学的修改，并给出了$L_2$光晕族中多次旋转转移的示例。

## Abstract
This paper explores the use of a reduced manifold space initialization method for the generation of both time and fuel optimal many-revolution, low-thrust transfers between three-body periodic orbits within a family. Libration point orbit families are approximated by multi-segment Chebyshev polynomials and 1-dimensional Fourier series. Control is mapped into the family space, and a 1-dimensional targeting scheme in the family space dynamics is used to generate an initial guess for the phase space, directly accounting for winding. An integral collocation-based direct method is then used to first continue a feasible trajectory into the phase space, and then optimize for a particular cost function. The methodology is first applied to the distant retrograde orbit family. Modifications for time-regularized dynamics are then discussed, with an ensuing example of many-revolution transfers in the $L_2$ halo family.

      ↓

- **关键创新**：将绕圈数（winding）作为显式设计变量处理，直接结合到降维瞄准中，从而避免了高维相空间中初值难猜、圈数不可控的问题。
