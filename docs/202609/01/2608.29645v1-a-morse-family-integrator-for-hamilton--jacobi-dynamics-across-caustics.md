## 摘要
我们发展了一个基于离散莫尔斯族、拉格朗日关系和Tulczyjew三元组的离散类似物的隐式离散哈密顿系统的几何框架。主要思想是将定义离散动力学的拉格朗日子流形视为基本几何对象，而不是显式的辛演化映射。这一观点自然地容纳了隐式、受约束和退化的离散系统。在此框架内，我们根据连续离散步骤之间拉格朗日子流形的传播，提出了第二类离散哈密顿-雅可比理论。当这些子流形局部由精确的1-形式dW_k和dW_{k+1}表示时，所得方程提供了连续生成函数之间的离散哈密顿-雅可比关系。更一般地，当到构型空间的投影变为奇异且单值生成函数不再存在时，我们表明演化可以通过第二类离散动力学与莫尔斯族的复合来描述。这为传播的拉格朗日子流形提供了一个生成族，而不需要将动力学表示为图。作为应用，我们考虑了光学波前穿过折叠焦散的传播。第二类离散哈密顿产生了一个辛射线积分器，而莫尔斯族表示焦散附近的多值波前。它们的复合提供了跨越奇点的完整拉格朗日子流形的离散传播规则。这样，相同的几何构造同时提供了离散哈密顿积分器和多值哈密顿-雅可比解的正则表示。

## Abstract
We develop a geometric framework for implicit discrete Hamiltonian systems based on discrete Morse families, Lagrangian relations, and discrete analogues of Tulczyjew's triple. The main idea is to regard the Lagrangian submanifold defining the discrete dynamics, rather than an explicit symplectic evolution map, as the fundamental geometric object. This viewpoint naturally accommodates implicit, constrained, and degenerate discrete systems.   Within this framework, we formulate a Type--II discrete Hamilton--Jacobi theory in terms of the propagation of Lagrangian submanifolds between consecutive discrete steps. When these submanifolds are locally represented by exact one-forms $dW_k$ and $dW_{k+1}$, the resulting equations provide a discrete Hamilton--Jacobi relation between consecutive generating functions. More generally, when the projection onto configuration space becomes singular and a single-valued generating function ceases to exist, we show that the evolution can be described by the composition of Type--II discrete dynamics with Morse families. This yields a generating family for the propagated Lagrangian submanifold without requiring the dynamics to be represented as a graph.   As an application, we consider the propagation of optical wavefronts through fold caustics. A Type--II discrete Hamiltonian yields a symplectic ray integrator, while a Morse family represents the multivalued wavefront near the caustic. Their composition provides a discrete propagation rule for the complete Lagrangian manifold across the singularity. In this way, the same geometric construction simultaneously provides a discrete Hamiltonian integrator and a regular representation of multivalued Hamilton--Jacobi solutions.
