## 摘要
高分辨率航天器图像为轨道修正提供了重要的天体测量约束，但对已分辨天体的测量往往受到劳动密集型的控制点选择以及在大规模图像档案中实现一致归算的困难所限制。我们提出了一种基于形状模型的火卫一自动化天体测量流程，并将其应用于火星快车高分辨率通道（SRC）图像。对于每次曝光，在名义航天器-目标-太阳几何关系下，利用高分辨率三维形状模型渲染合成图像。使用SuperPoint和SuperGlue建立观测图像与合成图像之间的特征对应关系，随后进行RANSAC滤波。然后，通过射线-形状相交将匹配的合成图像关键点与表面点关联起来。几何平差固定了采用的物体姿态、航天器状态和校正后的相机指向，并仅使用精确的透视投影模型估计两个有效的天空平面位置偏移。这些偏移用于推导火卫一的形心位置。我们首先在先前使用控制点方法分析过的图像集上测试该方法，获得了可比的天体测量性能。随后，我们将分析扩展到2007-2025年间更大的SRC数据集，获得了1113次成功的测量。相对于JPL MAR099星历，观测减计算残差的平均值在$α\times cos(δ)$方向为0.186 km，在$δ$方向为0.053 km，相应的标准差分别为0.609 km和0.583 km。这些结果表明，所提出的流程为大规模、均匀地归算火卫一档案航天器图像提供了一种实用方法，并具有应用于其他已分辨天体的潜力。

## 速览
**TLDR**：针对传统控制点方法劳动密集且难以一致处理大档案的问题，提出自动化基于形状模型的火卫一（Phobos）天测流程。利用SuperPoint/SuperGlue匹配真实与仿真图像，经RANSAC和射线求交后仅估计两个平面偏移。在2007-2025年SRC数据上获得1113次测量，残差标准差约0.6 km，展示了大规模同质化天测潜力。 \
**Motivation**：传统控制点选取费时且一致性差，限制了大批量航天图像的天测应用。 \
**Method**：渲染仿真图像，用SuperPoint/SuperGlue匹配并RANSAC，通过射线求交关联表面点，仅解算平面位置偏移。 \
**Result**：在2007-2025年SRC数据上成功测量1113次，相对MAR099残差均值0.186/0.053 km，标准差0.609/0.583 km。 \
**Conclusion**：该流程可高效实现火卫一大尺度同质天测，且可推广至其他有形状模型的天体。

## Abstract
High-resolution spacecraft images provide important astrometric constraints for orbit refinement, but measurements of resolved bodies are often limited by labor-intensive control-point selection and the difficulty of achieving consistent reductions over large image archives. We present an automated shape-model-based astrometric pipeline for Phobos and apply it to Mars Express Super Resolution Channel (SRC) images. For each exposure, a synthetic image is rendered from a high-resolution 3D shape model under the nominal spacecraft-target-Sun geometry. Feature correspondences between the observed and synthetic images are established using SuperPoint and SuperGlue, followed by RANSAC filtering. The matched synthetic-image keypoints are then associated with surface points through ray-shape intersection. The geometric adjustment fixes the adopted body orientation, spacecraft state, and corrected camera pointing and estimates only two effective plane-of-sky position offsets using the exact perspective-projection model. These offsets are used to derive the center-of-figure position of Phobos. We first test the method on an image set previously analysed with a control-point approach and obtain comparable astrometric performance. We then extend the analysis to a larger SRC dataset spanning 2007-2025 and obtain 1113 successful measurements. Relative to the JPL MAR099 ephemeris, the resulting observed-minus-computed residuals have mean values of 0.186 km in $α\times cos(δ)$ and 0.053 km in $δ$, with corresponding standard deviations of 0.609 km and 0.583 km. These results demonstrate that the proposed pipeline provides a practical approach to large-scale, homogeneous astrometric reduction of archival spacecraft images of Phobos, with potential application to other resolved bodies.
