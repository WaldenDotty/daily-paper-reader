## 摘要
许多物理现象自然地由受到随时间衰减的非自治扰动作用的动力系统所建模，包括激光脉冲与分子的相互作用、流行病学模型、非线性振荡系统以及天体力学。本文考虑哈密顿系统随时间衰减的依赖时间的扰动，该系统具有一个带有拟周期解的低维各向同性正常椭圆（相应地，双曲）不变环面。在扰动随时间具有适当衰减率的条件下，我们证明了扩展相空间中不变流形的存在性，并确定了相关横向动力学的渐近行为。上述结果针对Hölder、光滑和解析哈密顿系统获得。

## Abstract
Many physical phenomena are naturally modeled by dynamical systems subject to non-autonomous perturbations that decay in time, including the interaction of a laser pulse with a molecule, epidemiological models, nonlinear oscillatory systems, and celestial mechanics. In the present paper, we consider time-dependent perturbations decaying in time of Hamiltonian systems having a lower-dimensional isotropic normally elliptic (resp. hyperbolic) invariant torus with quasiperiodic solutions. Under suitable rates of decay in time of the perturbation, we prove the existence of invariant manifolds in the extended phase space, and determine the asymptotic behavior of the associated transverse dynamics. The above results are obtained for Hölder, smooth, and analytic Hamiltonian systems.
