## 摘要
本文提出了一种数据驱动的稳定流形（DD-SM）方法，该方法将库普曼算子表示学习与哈密顿-雅可比-贝尔曼（HJB）方程的几何稳定流形方法相结合，能够在无需系统动力学先验知识的情况下，从原始轨迹数据中实现端到端的最优反馈控制综合。我们在统一的对称子空间分解（SSD）和扩展动态模式分解（EDMD）框架下构建了一个增广控制系统，用于联合逼近漂移场、控制矩阵及其空间导数，并推导了不变和非不变字典空间的概率有限样本误差界，从而得到HJB方程的具有可证明准确性的近似特征系统。通过李雅普诺夫-佩龙算子和常微分方程扰动分析，我们证明了数据驱动的稳定流形随着训练数据的增加能够实现单调递减的半全局误差。我们进一步建立了闭环指数稳定性，并量化了最优性差距，这两者都可以通过提高模型精度来收紧。我们开发了一种具有自适应数据生成和深度神经网络逼近的高效算法流程，能够在1毫秒内输出控制信号。在改进的范德波尔振荡器上的实验验证了我们方法的有效性。

## Abstract
This paper presents a data-driven stable manifold (DD-SM) method, which integrates Koopman operator representation learning with the geometric stable manifold approach to Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations, enabling end-to-end optimal feedback control synthesis from raw trajectory data without prior knowledge of system dynamics.   We construct an augmented control system under a unified symmetric subspace decomposition (SSD) and extended dynamic mode decomposition (EDMD) framework for joint approximation of the drift field, control matrix and their spatial derivatives, and derive probabilistic finite-sample error bounds for invariant and non-invariant dictionary spaces to yield a provably accurate approximate characteristic system of HJB equation. Via Lyapunov-Perron operator and ODE perturbation analysis, we prove the data-driven stable manifold achieves monotonically decreasing semi-global error with growing training data. We further establish closed-loop exponential stability and quantify the optimality gap, both tightenable by refining model accuracy. An efficient algorithm pipeline with adaptive data generation and deep neural approximation is developed, outputting control signals within 1 millisecond. Experiments on a modified van der Pol oscillator verify the effectiveness of our method.
