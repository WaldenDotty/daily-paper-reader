## 摘要
压制性干扰、接收平台的高动态运动以及载波相位RTK使卫星导航面临一个基本问题：当接收机通过其阵列几何、通道状态、权重和信号处理规则主动参与观测形成时，由此产生的载波观测如何能在保持连续相位和整周模糊度结构的同时，继续代表同一客观传播过程？物理世界不会因观测者的重新配置而改变，但它在观测世界中的复数表示却会改变。如果没有严格区分传播引起的和观测者引起的变化，抗干扰、高动态和精密载波相位定位将在观测形成层面产生结构耦合。最后，针对静态干扰、动态旋转、支路间定时失配、接近零响应、射频重放和RTK整周风险验证，提供了可复现且可证伪的协议。SN-ASMO的限定新颖性在于将观测形成、参考协方差、精确冗余商、同源传输、内在统计信息以及RTK整数结构保持统一在一个数学上封闭且物理上可测试的框架中。

## Abstract
Suppressive interference, high-dynamic motion of the receiving platform, and carrier-phase RTK lead satellite navigation to one fundamental question: when the receiver actively participates in observation formation through its array geometry, channel states, weights, and signal-processing rules, how can the resulting carrier observation continue to represent the same objective propagation process while preserving continuous phase and the integer-ambiguity structure? The physical world is not changed by a reconfiguration of the observer, but its complex representation in the observation world is. Without a strict separation between propagation-induced and observer-induced variations, anti-jamming, high dynamics, and precise carrier-phase positioning become structurally coupled at the observation-formation level.   Finally, reproducible and falsifiable protocols are provided for static interference, dynamic rotation, inter-branch timing mismatch, near-null response, RF replay, and RTK integer-risk validation. The scoped novelty of SN-ASMO is the unification of observation formation, reference covariance, exact nuisance quotients, same-source transport, intrinsic statistical information, and preservation of the RTK integer structure in one mathematically closed and physically testable framework.
