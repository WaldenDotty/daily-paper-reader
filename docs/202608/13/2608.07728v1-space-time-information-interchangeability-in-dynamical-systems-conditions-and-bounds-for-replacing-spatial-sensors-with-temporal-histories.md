## 摘要
许多动态状态重构问题旨在仅凭少数位置的测量来推断高维空间状态。由于控制动力学将跨空间和时间的演化耦合在一起，时间测量可以包含传感器位置之外的状态分量信息。本文发展了一个理论框架，比较时间测量历史与指定瞬时空间传感器阵列的信息内容。时间历史和空间参考阵列由先验白化的信息算子表示，这些算子考虑了先验变异性、传感器几何和测量噪声，同时保持不确定性缩减的方向分布。一个一般的时空信息交换定理给出了在每个状态方向上保留指定比例的空间参考信息的充要条件。它回答了三个实际问题。第一，需要多少次时间测量以及多少总信息？第二，指定比例是否可达到，还是有限的历史会因信息上限而被排除？第三，当可达到时，需要多长的历史？针对收缩的、谱分离酉的以及可同时对角化的耗散线性动力学，以及高斯过程和测量噪声下的随机线性估计，推导了显式的历史长度界。对于非线性系统，将全局延迟映射的单射性与一致局部Fisher信息条件相结合。数值实验测试了方向性交换长度、信息上限和有限记忆界限，并评估了非线性延迟映射的可区分性和局部Fisher信息预测。该框架为确定时间测量何时能够减少全状态重构的空间传感器覆盖范围提供了明确的基础。

## Abstract
Many dynamical-state reconstruction problems seek to infer a high-dimensional spatial state from measurements at only a few locations. Because the governing dynamics couple evolution across space and time, temporal measurements can contain information about state components beyond the sensor locations. This work develops a theoretical framework comparing the information content of a temporal measurement history with that of a specified instantaneous spatial sensor array. The temporal history and spatial reference array are represented by prior-whitened information operators that account for prior variability, sensor geometry, and measurement noise while preserving the directional distribution of uncertainty reduction. A general space-time information-exchange theorem gives necessary and sufficient conditions for retaining a prescribed fraction of the spatial-reference information in every state direction. It answers three practical questions. First, how many temporal measurements and how much total information are required? Second, is the prescribed fraction attainable, or does a limiting information ceiling rule out every finite history? Third, when attainable, what history length is sufficient? Explicit history-length bounds are derived for contractive, spectrally separated unitary, and simultaneously diagonalizable dissipative linear dynamics, and for stochastic linear estimation under Gaussian process and measurement noise. For nonlinear systems, global delay-map injectivity is combined with a uniform local Fisher-information condition. Numerical experiments test directional exchange lengths, information ceilings, and finite-memory bounds, and assess nonlinear delay-map distinguishability and local Fisher-information predictions. The framework provides an explicit basis for determining when temporal measurements can reduce spatial sensor coverage for full-state reconstruction.
