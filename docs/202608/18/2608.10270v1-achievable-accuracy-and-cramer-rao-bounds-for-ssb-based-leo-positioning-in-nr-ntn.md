## 摘要
先进的低地球轨道（LEO）卫星网络，如Starlink移动卫星服务（MSS），将采用5G新空口（NR）非地面网络（NTN）标准。这使得利用无处不在的同步信号块（SSB）基于伪距和多普勒测量进行机会性接收机定位成为可能。在本工作中，我们通过推导与伪距和多普勒相关的延迟和载波频率观测量的单一SSB Cramer Rao下界（CRLBs），刻画了基于SSB定位的估计理论极限。这些界限利用完整的SSB时频能量分布获得，并扩展到多历元、多卫星的Fisher信息框架中，该框架联合约束了静止用户位置、时钟偏差和时钟漂移。每个SSB的贡献根据由接收信噪比确定的距离相关CRLB进行加权，因此估计器与界限共享相同的噪声模型。在仿真中，所得界限与可实现性能紧密匹配，且物理加权最小二乘估计器在实际工作信噪比下接近CRLB。使用基于Starlink的星座，我们分析了地面用户所经历的工作信噪比，并展示了亚米级定位精度。

## Abstract
Advanced Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite networks, such as Starlinks Mobile Satellite Service (MSS), will adopt the 5G New Radio (NR) Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) standard. This enables the use of ubiquitous Synchronization Signal Blocks (SSBs) for opportunistic receiver positioning based on pseudorange and Doppler measurements. In this work, we characterize the estimation theoretic limits of SSB-based positioning by deriving single SSB Cramer Rao lower bounds (CRLBs) for delay and carrier frequency observables associated with pseudorange and Doppler. These bounds are obtained using the full SSB time-frequency energy distribution and extended into a multi epoch, multi satellite Fisher information framework that jointly bounds stationary user position, clock bias, and clock drift. Each SSB contribution is weighted according to a range dependent CRLB determined by the received SNR, so the estimator and the bound share a common noise model. In simulation, the resulting bound closely matches achievable performance, and a physically weighted least squares estimator approaches the CRLB at realistic operating SNR. Using a Starlink based constellation, we analyze the operating SNR experienced by a ground user and demonstrate sub-meter positioning accuracy.
