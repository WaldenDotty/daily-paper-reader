## 摘要
椭圆月球冻结轨道（ELFO）是支持月球南极探测的月球卫星星座的有吸引力的候选方案。在先前基于频率的轨道分析方法的基础上，本研究开发了一个基于环面、频域的框架，用于星座级ELFO的设计与分析。在双平均动力学模型中，每个ELFO由三个频率-角度对表征；通过跨渐进更高保真度模型的系统比较，识别了在每个级别引入的额外频率分量。角度参数化具有多种对称性，可显著减小设计空间，从而能够快速、无历元地全局调查星座构型。随后，一个更高保真度的细化和分析层结合了（1）频域微分校正器（FDDC），用于瞄准期望的频率特性并抑制不希望的长周期振荡，（2）傅里叶代理重优化，用于无需在循环中传播的星间定相，以及（3）频谱归因，将可通过设计细化的覆盖损失与动力学固有的覆盖损失分开。这些元素共同为ELFO星座设计与分析提供了一个保真度桥接和诊断框架。

## Abstract
Elliptical Lunar Frozen Orbits (ELFOs) are attractive candidates for lunar satellite constellations supporting lunar south pole exploration. Building on prior frequency-based orbit analysis methods, this investigation develops a torus-based, frequency-domain framework for constellation-level ELFO design and analysis. Within a doubly-averaged dynamical model, each ELFO is characterized by three frequency-angle pairs; a systematic comparison across progressively higher-fidelity models identifies the additional frequency components introduced at each level. The angular parametrization admits multiple symmetries that substantially reduce the design space, enabling a rapid, epoch-free global survey of constellation configurations. A higher-fidelity refinement and analysis layer then combines a (1) Frequency-Domain Differential Corrector (FDDC) that targets desired frequency properties and suppresses undesired long-period oscillations, (2) Fourier surrogate re-optimization for inter-satellite phasing without propagation in the loop, and (3) spectral attribution that separates design-refinable coverage losses from those inherent to the dynamics. Together, these elements supply a fidelity-bridging and diagnostic framework for ELFO constellation design and analysis.
