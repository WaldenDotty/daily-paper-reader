## 摘要
尽管用于哈密顿系统正向模拟的保结构方法已经成熟，但其数据同化与滤波对应方法的数学基础仍相对不够完善，尤其是在严格的精度保证方面。为弥补这一空白，我们提出一种在线滤波算法，在假设未知函数 u† 求解一个含未知输入的参数化哈密顿偏微分方程（PDE）的情况下，从有限多个测量中重构该未知函数。该方法使用低维辛近似空间，这些空间依据 PDE 模型随时间演化，并由测量数据提供信息。该方法还可以与一种动态传感器布置策略相结合，该策略旨在优化重构稳定性。我们推导了基于稳定性和最佳逼近的误差界，并表明重构结果仍位于辛空间中，且能量在近似误差范围内保持守恒。数值实验证明了该方法的有效性，以及动态传感器布置相较于静态布置的优势。

## Abstract
While structure-preserving methods for the forward simulation of Hamiltonian systems are well established, the mathematical foundations of their data assimilation and filtering counterparts remain comparatively less developed, particularly regarding rigorous accuracy guarantees. To address this gap, we propose an online filtering algorithm to reconstruct an unknown function $u^\dagger$ from finitely many measurements, assuming that $u^\dagger$ solves a parametric Hamiltonian PDE with unknown inputs. The method uses low-dimensional symplectic approximation spaces that evolve in time according to the PDE model and informed by the measurement data. The method can also be coupled with a dynamical sensor-placement strategy designed to optimize reconstruction stability. We derive error bounds in terms of stability and best approximation, and show that the reconstruction remains in a symplectic space and preserves energy up to the approximation error. Numerical experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of the method and the benefits of dynamic over static sensor placement.
