## 摘要
航天器交会与近距离操作（RPO）目前通过高度依赖专业知识的流程进行规划，其中工程师将高层任务意图转化为安全且动力学可行的轨迹，这成为可扩展操作的瓶颈。基于大语言模型（LLM）的智能体可为该流程提供直观接口，尽管其输出本质上并非以轨道动力学、操作约束或可允许航天器机动的结构为依据。为利用其语义推理能力，同时确保生成计划的物理有效性，本文提出了一种用于航天器任务与运动规划（TAMP）的分层框架，将LLM推理锚定于可复用行为图与领域特定规划模块。在该框架中，预训练LLM将自然语言指令映射为部分任务规范。相关规划器随后在可允许的操作空间内解决未指定的决策。最后，轨迹优化将补全后的任务规范转换为动力学可行的轨迹。数值实验表明，与直接LLM生成相比，该架构显著提升了意图恢复，在由前沿LLM支持时，在所有评估划分上对部分任务规范实现了98%的精确恢复。额外的测试时计算实验表明，对于紧凑的9B模型，验证器引导的修正将精确恢复率从75%提高到88%，而更广泛的行为规划搜索则独立地改善了在可允许轨迹实现之间的选择。总体而言，这些结果为航天器RPO的语言驱动智能体规划建立了可扩展且可审计的基础。

## Abstract
Spacecraft rendezvous and proximity operations (RPO) are currently planned through an expertise-intensive process in which engineers translate high-level operational intent into safe, dynamically feasible trajectories, creating a bottleneck to scalable operations. Large language model (LLM)-based agents could offer an intuitive interface for this process, although their outputs are not inherently grounded in orbital dynamics, operational constraints, or the structure of admissible spacecraft maneuvers. To exploit their semantic reasoning while ensuring the generated plan's physical validity, this paper presents a hierarchical framework for spacecraft task-and-motion planning (TAMP) that grounds LLM reasoning in a graph of reusable behaviors and domain-specific planning modules. Within this framework, a pretrained LLM maps a natural-language command to a partial mission specification. The associated planners then resolve unspecified decisions within the admissible operational space. Finally, trajectory optimization converts the completed mission specification into a dynamically feasible trajectory. Numerical experiments demonstrate that this architecture substantially improves intent recovery over direct LLM generation, achieving 98% exact recovery of partial mission specifications across all evaluated splits when backed by frontier LLMs. Additional test-time-compute experiments show that, for a compact 9B model, verifier-guided revision increases exact recovery from 75% to 88%, while broader behavior-plan search independently improves selection among admissible trajectory realizations. Overall, these results establish a scalable and auditable foundation for language-driven agentic planning of spacecraft RPO.
