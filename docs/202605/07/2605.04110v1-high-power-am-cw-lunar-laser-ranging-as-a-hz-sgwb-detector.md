## 摘要
地月双星系统是月球轨道频率谐波处随机引力波背景（SGWB）的共振探测器。我们将高功率幅度调制连续波月球激光测距（AM-CW LLR）量化为微赫兹（$μ$Hz）SGWB 探测手段。主要的低偏心率响应位于 $f_2=2/P_{\rm M}=0.847245\,μ{\rm Hz}$。AM-CW LLR 测量由月球角反射器反射的 GHz 调制 1064 nm 光载波上的射频相位，从而提供距离和距离变化率观测值。在 $80\,μ{\rm m}$ 的绝对距离不确定度下，一项为期 5 年、统计独立的 AM-CW 相位标准点速率为 $ν_{\rm eff}=500\,{\rm yr}^{-1}$ 的观测计划，其响应校准灵敏度为 $Ω_{\rm gw}^{95}=5.29\times10^{-9}D_{\rm cov}$；成熟实施方案在 $σ_R=50\,μ{\rm m}$ 时可达 $2.07\times10^{-9}D_{\rm cov}$，其中 $D_{\rm cov}\ge1$ 是全局解中时间相关残差和冗余参数相关性的协方差退化因子。预期的初级相变和致密双星信号在 $80\,μ{\rm m}$ 情况下分别对于 $D_{\rm cov}\lesssim3.6$ 和 $5.4$ 高于标称的 5-$σ$ 协方差振幅阈值，而在 $50\,μ{\rm m}$ 情况下则分别对于 $D_{\rm cov}\lesssim9.1$ 和 $13.7$ 高于该阈值。因此，该实验是一项严苛的协方差测试：绝对距离携带 SGWB 信号，而距离变化率和多反射器差分数据则决定了冗余相关性是否能使 $D_{\rm cov}$ 保持在发现余量之下。

## Abstract
The Earth--Moon binary is a resonant detector for stochastic gravitational-wave background (SGWB) at harmonics of the lunar orbital frequency. We quantify high-power amplitude-modulated continuous-wave lunar laser ranging (AM-CW LLR) as a $μ$Hz SGWB probe. The dominant low-eccentricity response is at $f_2=2/P_{\rm M}=0.847245\,μ{\rm Hz}$. AM-CW LLR measures radio-frequency phase on a GHz-modulated 1064 nm optical carrier reflected by lunar corner cubes, giving range and range rate observables. With an $80\,μ{\rm m}$ absolute range uncertainty, a 5-year campaign with statistically independent AM-CW phase-normal-point rate of $ν_{\rm eff}=500\,{\rm yr}^{-1}$ has response-calibrated sensitivity $Ω_{\rm gw}^{95}=5.29\times10^{-9}D_{\rm cov}$; a mature implementation with $σ_R=50\,μ{\rm m}$ gives $2.07\times10^{-9}D_{\rm cov}$, where $D_{\rm cov}\ge1$ is a covariance-degradation factor for time-correlated residuals and nuisance-parameter correlations in the global solution. Anticipated first-order phase-transition and compact-binary signals lie above the nominal 5-$σ$ covariance-amplitude threshold for $D_{\rm cov}\lesssim3.6$ and $5.4$, respectively, in the $80\,μ{\rm m}$ case, and for $D_{\rm cov}\lesssim9.1$ and $13.7$ in the $50\,μ{\rm m}$ case. Thus the experiment is a sharp covariance test: absolute range carries the SGWB signal, while range rate and multi-reflector differential data determine whether nuisance correlations keep $D_{\rm cov}$ below the discovery margins.
